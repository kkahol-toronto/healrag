=== FINAL ASSEMBLED RESPONSE ===
If you lose your laptop, you should take the following steps as outlined in the provided documentation:

1. **Report the Incident Immediately:** Notify your line manager and the IT Service Desk as soon as possible. This is crucial for initiating the appropriate response and minimizing potential risks.

2. **Incident Management:** The loss of a laptop is considered a security incident and should be reported in accordance with the organization's Cyber & Information Security Incident Management Standard. This ensures that the incident is logged, investigated, and managed properly [[C&IS 002_Cyber & Information Security Incident Management Standard.md]].

3. **Follow IT and Security Guidance:** The IT Service Desk or Information Security team may provide further instructions, such as remotely disabling or wiping the device, changing passwords, or monitoring for unauthorized access.

4. **Document the Incident:** Be prepared to provide details about the lost laptop, such as the last known location, time of loss, and any sensitive information it may have contained.

Following these steps helps protect company data and ensures compliance with security policies. If you need more specific guidance, refer to your organization's incident management procedures or contact your IT department directly [[C&IS 002_Cyber & Information Security Incident Management Standard.md]].
